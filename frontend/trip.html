How to use

1. Add expenses

Tap "+ Add expense" in the Activity tab. Enter what it was for, the amount, who paid, and how to split it — equal, percentage, or exact amounts.

2. Check balances

Open the People tab to see who owes and who should receive money. Green means owed money back, orange means owes money.

3. Settle up

Go to the Settle tab to see the minimum set of payments needed to clear everything.

4. Share the trip

Tap Share to get an edit link (full access) or a view-only link, and send it to the group.
